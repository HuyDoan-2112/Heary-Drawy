# 10 - Bot partner

Status: **deferred - not part of the demo**

The demo always has exactly two human players.

## Later (deferred)

When an odd number of players join, pair the extra player with a bot that can play either role. A bot Speaker would use prerecorded descriptions; a bot Drawer would turn the child's spoken clues into simple shapes and must never see the secret picture. Depends on transcription ([06](06-transcription.md)).
