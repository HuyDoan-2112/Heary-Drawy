# Heary Drawy

A cooperative communication-practice game in which teammates describe private objects and draw what they hear. This glossary names the people, shared activity, and feedback without prescribing implementation.

## Language

**Room**:
An adult-managed private group of admitted participants from which teammates are matched.
_Avoid_: Public lobby, voice channel

**Pair**:
Two teammates within one Room. One may be a Bot Partner when the Room has an odd participant.
_Avoid_: Opponents

**Match**:
A sequence of Rounds played by the same Pair, with shared team results and alternating roles.
_Avoid_: Round when referring to the entire session

**Round**:
One hidden-object activity, ending with role-specific feedback and a Discovery Reward before the next role swap.
_Avoid_: Match when referring to one object

**Speaker**:
The teammate who sees and explores the Secret Object, describes it without naming it, and can guide the Drawer by watching the evolving drawing.
_Avoid_: Describer in new specifications; it is the older research term for the same role

**Drawer**:
The teammate who draws their interpretation of the Speaker's clues and asks for clarification, without seeing the Secret Object before Reveal.

**Nickname**:
The participant's chosen display name within a Room, not a required real name or proof of who they are.

**Spoken Turn**:
One contribution spoken while a teammate holds their talk button; either role may speak during collaboration and contributions may overlap.
_Avoid_: Recording, unrestricted chat

**Secret Object**:
The reference object available to Speaker during preparation and drawing, but hidden from Drawer until Reveal.

**Drawing**:
The Drawer's evolving interpretation of the spoken clues, visible to both teammates but editable only by Drawer.

**Feature**:
A curated trait of the Secret Object used to discuss what was communicated or represented, such as color, shape, or a part. Not every useful spoken feature is drawable.

**Feature Evidence**:
A detected, missing, or uncertain observation about a Feature in speech or Drawing; not a diagnosis or proof of ability.

**Role Feedback**:
A role-specific recap and gentle suggestion after a Round: communication evidence for Speaker, represented communicated features for Drawer.
_Avoid_: Individual ranking, artistic grade

**Reveal**:
The post-submission result stage in which both teammates may see the Secret Object and compare it with the Drawing.

**Discovery Reward**:
The interactive 3D exploration offered after every Round's feedback regardless of stars, before Continue and the role swap.

**Bot Partner**:
A simulated teammate that follows the same role and hidden-information rules as a human teammate.
_Avoid_: Answer oracle
